## `crm_collections`

**CRM payments: view dues and collections** · Read only

See what a lead owes, who is overdue, what falls due soon, converted leads who have not paid and failed checkouts — for the people behind the leads each user can see. Admins also see money collected, by day, method, course and counsellor.

| Action | What it does | Changes data | How it applies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `lead_dues` | One lead's money here: enrolments with fees billed / paid / due, the installment schedule with overdue flags, pending invoices and the last payments (references masked). | No | — |
| `who_owes` | People with overdue (or any outstanding) installments, invoices or renewals: amount, oldest due date, days overdue, counsellor, course; paged, contacts masked. | No | — |
| `due_soon` | Installments and invoices falling due in the next N days (default 7, max 60). | No | — |
| `collections` | Money collected in a window: totals, by day, by method and offline mode, by course, by counsellor, by currency; optional previous-period comparison. *(institute admins only)* | No | — |
| `converted_not_paid` | People converted in the window with no PAID payment to this institute. | No | — |
| `abandoned_checkouts` | People whose payment attempt failed or never completed in the window, with no later successful payment here. | No | — |

## `crm_payment_reminders`

**CRM payments: send fee reminders** · Makes changes · Calls or messages leads, or sends them invites — always after you confirm. · Turned on separately (opt-in)

Let the connected AI app send fee reminders for unpaid installments or pending invoices — through your installment-due automations or the invoice reminder. Admins only; shown to you first and sent only after you confirm, at most 50 an hour. Turned on separately.

<Note>
**What keeps it safe:** outbound, admins only, single-use confirmed: reminds at most 50 unpaid installments or pending invoices verified to belong to this institute through admin-core's own reminder endpoints (the institute's installment-due automations; the invoice's in-app alert and email); refuses paid, cancelled and waived bills, inactive enrolments and opted-out people; the apply sends exactly the previewed items, re-checked; capped at 50 reminders per user per hour
</Note>

| Action | What it does | Changes data | How it applies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `send_installment_reminder` | Remind one or up to 50 unpaid installments through the institute's installment-due automations. *(institute admins only)* | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |
| `send_invoice_reminder` | Remind one or up to 50 pending invoices (in-app alert, and email when on). *(institute admins only)* | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |
| `quota` | How many reminders you can still confirm this hour. | No | — |
