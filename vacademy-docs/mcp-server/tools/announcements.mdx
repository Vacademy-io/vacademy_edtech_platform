## `send_announcement`

**Announcements: send** · Makes changes · Calls or messages people (leads, or learners and staff through an announcement), or sends them invites — always after you confirm. · Turned on separately (opt-in)

Let the connected AI app send an announcement to batches, roles or chosen learners as an in-app alert, optionally with a push notification. It always shows you who receives it and the text first and sends only after you confirm; each confirmation works once and sends per hour are capped. Turned on separately.

<Note>
**What keeps it safe:** outbound, single-use confirmed: sends one announcement (in-app alert, optionally a push notification) to batches and learners verified to belong to this institute, or to a whole role here; the institute and sender are always the connected user, never arguments; needs a single-use confirm_token from a preview of that exact title, text and recipient list, and counts toward an hourly cap per user
</Note>

<ParamField path="message" type="string" required>
