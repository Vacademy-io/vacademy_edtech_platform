## `crm_calls`

**CRM calls: view** · Read only

See calls with leads — human and AI calls, their outcomes, transcripts and summaries — plus callbacks due, missed inbound calls, and the AI calling agents, queue and campaign runs.

## `crm_ai_campaign`

**CRM calls: AI-call campaigns** · Makes changes · Calls or messages leads, or sends them invites — always after you confirm. · Turned on separately (opt-in)

Let the connected AI app start AI-calling campaigns for up to 500 leads at a time, and cancel a run or pause the AI call queue. Admins only. It shows you exactly who will be called (and who was left out, and why) and the estimated credits before you confirm. Turned on separately.

<Note>
**What keeps it safe:** outbound, admins only, single-use confirmed: AI-calls at most 500 leads resolved in the caller's scope and listed in the preview (opted-out, converted, lost, do-not-call and recently contacted leads are dropped with a reason); the apply sends exactly that stored list, re-checked against the scope, never an empty or whole list; cancelling a run or pausing / resuming the queue acts only on runs verified to belong to this institute
</Note>

| Action | What it does | Changes data | How it applies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `start` | AI-call up to 500 leads from a filter or lead ids, across lists, with one confirm. *(institute admins only)* | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |
| `cancel_run` | Cancel the calls of one campaign run that are still waiting. *(institute admins only)* | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |
| `pause_queue` | Hold the institute's AI call queue (nothing is cancelled). *(institute admins only)* | Yes | Saved straight away |
| `resume_queue` | Release a held AI call queue so waiting calls start dialling again. *(institute admins only)* | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |

## `crm_dial`

**CRM calls: place calls** · Makes changes · Calls or messages leads, or sends them invites — always after you confirm. · Turned on separately (opt-in)

Let the connected AI app call a lead for you (your phone rings first, then the lead's) or have an AI agent call one lead. Each call is shown to you first and placed only after you confirm; opted-out and do-not-call leads are refused, and calls can use telephony or AI credits. Turned on separately.

<Note>
**What keeps it safe:** outbound, single-use confirmed: places ONE call (the caller's own phone rings first) or queues ONE AI call to a lead in the caller's scope, to the number on the lead's record; refuses opted-out and do-not-call leads, AI calls outside calling hours or inside the recall gaps; each call needs a single-use confirm_token from a preview of that exact lead and counts toward an hourly cap; cancel_queued cancels only the caller's own queued items
</Note>

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `call` | Click-to-call one lead: the caller's own phone rings first, then the lead's. | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |
| `ai_call` | Have an AI agent call one lead now (inside calling hours and the recall limits). | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |
| `cancel_queued` | Cancel one AI call that is still waiting in the queue (your own, unless admin). | Yes | Saved straight away |
| `quota` | How many calls and AI calls you can still confirm this hour. | No | — |
