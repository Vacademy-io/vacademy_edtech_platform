## `crm_messages`

**CRM messages: view** · Read only

See a lead's WhatsApp and email history, the WhatsApp inbox, your approved message templates, whether a lead can be messaged, and conversations escalated to staff.

| Action | What it does | Changes data | How it applies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `history` | One lead's WhatsApp and email messages in this institute, newest first. | No | — |
| `inbox` | WhatsApp conversations (all, unanswered or failed) for the leads you can see. | No | — |
| `templates` | Approved WhatsApp templates (language, header, variables) and email templates (id, subject, variables) you can send with crm_send. | No | — |
| `can_message` | Pre-flight for one lead: opt-out; WhatsApp number, channel and 24-hour window; email address, unsubscribe list and sender limits; sends left this hour. | No | — |
| `escalations` | Chatbot conversations handed over to staff, for the leads you can see. | No | — |

## `crm_send`

**CRM messages: send WhatsApp and email** · Makes changes · Calls or messages leads, or sends them invites — always after you confirm. · Turned on separately (opt-in)

Let the connected AI app send one of your approved WhatsApp templates, or an email (one of your email templates or one written with you), to one lead at a time. Each message is shown to you first and sent only after you confirm; opted-out leads and unsubscribed addresses are refused and sends per hour are capped. Turned on separately.

<Note>
**What keeps it safe:** outbound, single-use confirmed: sends ONE approved WhatsApp template or ONE email (an institute email template or a subject and body written with the user) to ONE lead in the caller's scope, to the number or address on the lead's record (never one typed in the chat); refuses opted-out leads and addresses on the institute's email unsubscribe list; needs a single-use confirm_token from a preview of that exact message and counts toward an hourly cap per user (WhatsApp and email counted separately)
</Note>

| Action | What it does | Changes data | How it applies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `send` | Send one approved WhatsApp template, or one email (a template or a written subject and body), to one lead (preview, then confirm). | Yes | Preview, then your yes (single use) |
| `quota` | WhatsApp sends and emails you have left this hour. | No | — |
