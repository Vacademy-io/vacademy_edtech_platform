**Active connections** in **Settings → MCP Server** lists every AI app that is signed in to your institute right now. Admins can see and revoke all of them.

## What each row shows

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| App name | The name the AI app gave itself when it registered, for example the name of the AI app, or the name of a [client ID](/mcp-server/settings/client-ids) you created. |
| **Connected by** | The person who approved the connection. The app acts as this person. |
| **Last used** | When the app last made a request. **Never used** means it signed in but has not used a tool yet. |
| **Revoke** | Ends this connection. |

One row is one person signed in with one app. If a person connects Claude and Cursor, you see two rows.

A connection leaves the list when it is revoked, or when it has not been used for 30 days and its sign-in expires. See [Tokens and expiry](/mcp-server/oauth/tokens-and-expiry).

## Revoke a connection

Click **Revoke** on the row. The connection ends at once: the app's next request is refused, and the person has to sign in again from their app.

<Note>
  Revoking ends one sign-in. It does not stop the person from connecting again. If they still hold an allowed role, they can reconnect. To keep someone out, also remove their role from [Who can connect](/mcp-server/settings/roles), or change their role in your team settings.
</Note>

## All the ways to cut access

| You want to stop | Do this | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| One person's app | **Revoke** their row | Their next request |
| Everyone with a role | Remove the role from **Who can connect**, then save | Each app's next request |
| One area or one change, for everyone | Set the area to **Off** or switch the change off in the main list **and** in every customized role under **Per-role access**, then save | Each app's next request |
| Everything | Turn off **MCP server enabled**, then save | Each app's next request |

Settings changes do not delete connections. When a role, area or the whole server is turned back on, apps that are still signed in work again. Revoke the connections too if you want everyone to sign in again.

## What the person sees

- A **revoked** connection: their app is no longer signed in. Most apps then ask them to sign in again.
- A **switched-off** server or a **removed role**: the app's list of tools is empty, and using a tool returns a message such as *"The MCP server is not enabled for this institute"* or *"This user's role is not allowed to connect to the MCP server"*, with a pointer to the MCP settings.
- An area set to **Off**: its tools disappear from the app's list.

## When someone leaves your team

1. Revoke every row where **Connected by** shows them.
2. Remove or change their role in your team settings.

Do the same when someone changes role: revoke their rows, and they reconnect with their new role.

## Disconnecting from the app side

A person can also remove the server from their AI app. Some apps tell the server to end the sign-in when they do that; others do not. To be sure a connection is gone, check **Active connections** and revoke it there.
