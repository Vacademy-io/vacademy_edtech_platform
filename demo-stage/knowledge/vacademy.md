## What Vacademy is

Vacademy is one platform for running an education institute: admissions and
CRM, courses and live classes, tests and AI evaluation, fees, messaging,
reports, and the institute's own branded website and apps. One login and one
learner record across all of it.

Vacademy serves four goals an institute owner has: Grow (fill batches), Teach
(deliver learning), Keep (keep learners and parents close) and Run (run the
institute with less effort). There are fifteen product areas under these.

Who it is for: coaching institutes and online academies, exam preparation
(NEET, JEE, UPSC, IELTS and similar), schools, universities running distance
learning, and corporate training teams.

## The product family

Six products run on the same platform and can also be used on their own:
Vacademy LMS (complete learning platform, vacademy.io), VoomSales (admissions
CRM, voomsales.com), Examezy (exams and assessments, examezy.com), Evalezy (AI
answer-sheet evaluation, evalezy.com), Tutezy (personal AI tutor, tutezy.ai)
and Telleo (AI calling, telleo.ai). An institute can start with one and switch
on the rest later.

## Grow: admissions, website, calling

Leads from ads, the website, walk-ins and QR forms land in one de-duplicated
list. Meta and Google lead forms connect directly.

Leads are assigned to counsellors by round-robin, by team and shift.

Follow-ups: due tasks pop up for the counsellor, and overdue ones alert the
manager. The dashboard shows the lead pipeline and follow-up health.

The admission pipeline is a drag-and-drop board from enquiry to enrolled.

Lead scoring is automatic and rule-based (the institute sets the weights and
its own interest levels). It is not an AI score.

Online admissions: enrolment links with forms, documents, approvals and
payment. Demos and visits can be booked online with booking pages and
reminders.

Counsellor performance: targets, ratings and a team leaderboard. Leads can be
tagged and reported by centre and branch.

AI calling (also sold as Telleo): an AI voice agent calls new leads within
minutes at any hour, in Indian languages, qualifies them and books demos or
visits, and retries if the call is not answered. The script can be written by
AI from the lead's details. Bulk AI calling campaigns can be paused and
resumed.

Every call is recorded with an AI transcript, a summary, the next step and a
conversion likelihood, plus coaching tips for counsellors.

Counsellors can click to call from the lead page; calls are logged. An AI
receptionist, phone menus and routing handle incoming calls.

AI website builder: describe the institute and AI drafts the whole site; any
section can be regenerated. Sites have SEO-ready pages, a blog, course pages
with cart, offers and checkout, coupons with limits and dates, multi-language
pages and printable QR codes for campaign forms.

A website AI assistant can answer visitors' questions about courses on the
institute's own site and take enquiries. The institute switches it on.

Email follow-ups to leads can be written by AI. WhatsApp uses approved
templates and a shared team inbox.

## Teach: courses, live classes, tests, AI

Courses hold video, PDF, quizzes, code and more in one player. Content can be
bulk uploaded (folders, zips, SCORM), released on a schedule (by date, by day
or by progress), and protected with copy, download and device controls.
Mobile apps support encrypted offline downloads. Certificates are issued on
completion with a QR code that verifies them.

Daily engagement: a Today screen with lessons, flashcards and quick tasks,
with streaks, points and catch-up rules. Plans can be drafted with AI.

Live classes run on Zoom, Google Meet or the built-in classroom, on one
schedule. Attendance is automatic with minimum-time rules. Recordings are
published into the right chapter. Live polls and quizzes run in class. Paid
public classes (webinars) have their own registration. Learners rate classes.

Tests: online, offline, OMR and coding tests. AI drafts question papers by
syllabus and weightage, and turns PDFs of papers into tests. Online exams
have camera proctoring and tab-switch tracking, and a live view of who is
writing, stuck or flagged. Results, rankings and report cards follow.

Exam scale: the platform has been load-tested with 3,000 learners taking an
exam at the same time.

AI evaluation (also sold as Evalezy): AI checks handwritten and typed long
answers. A whole class's scanned sheets can be uploaded at once and matched
to names. Results are held until a teacher approves them. The cost is shown
before checking starts.

Course AI: describe a course and AI drafts the outline, pages, videos, notes
and quizzes for the teacher to review.

AI video studio: a prompt or script becomes a finished explainer video, with
AI voiceover, optional avatar, translation into other languages, and cuts for
reels. Brand kits keep colours, fonts and intros consistent.

AI tutor (also sold as Tutezy): teaches one to one on a whiteboard with
voice, checks understanding and re-teaches. A teacher's own voice can be used.

Student chatbot: a study assistant inside each lesson that answers from the
institute's own course material, including the PDF on screen. Admins choose
where learners can use it.

Knowledge base: upload books once, then ask questions and build tests from
them.

Teachers get lesson plans, class notes and quizzes generated for each
lecture, including notes from class recordings.

Staff can ask an assistant in plain words to find things or do tasks (for
example "who owes fees this week"). Institutes can also connect their own AI
app (Claude, ChatGPT and others) through MCP with permissions they set.

## Keep: messaging and community

Announcements can be targeted by batch, role or tag, with delivery tracking,
and scheduled or recurring. Email can be sent from the institute's own
domain. Class reminders go by email, push notification and in-app.

A doubt board gives every doubt an owner and a status. Batch chat has
moderation.

Badges, points, streaks and leaderboards reward progress. Mentors can be
matched to learners for one-to-one sessions.

## Run: fees, operations, reports, automations

Fees: online collection through major payment gateways, payment links,
instalment plans with automatic reminders, branded tax invoices, dues and
overdue ageing, renewals with reminders and grace periods, and discount rules
that need approval and are logged. AI calls can remind parents about fees.

Operations: one record per learner (profile, batches, attendance,
documents), bulk enrolment from a spreadsheet with a preview, a parent
portal, branches as sub-organisations with their own admins, and custom roles
and permissions.

HR: staff attendance, leave, payroll and payslips.

Reports: a live dashboard, admissions funnel by source, counsellor, teacher
and batch reports, at-risk learner flags, an audit log, and daily, weekly or
monthly email digests.

Automations: visual workflows triggered by real events, ready-made recipes,
and workflows drafted by AI from plain words. A workflow step can place an AI
call.

## Your brand

Institutes get their own domain and branded sign-in, their colours, fonts and
terms, and their own Android, iOS, Windows and Mac apps in the stores under
their name. The interface and courses can be translated.

## Security

Vacademy holds ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certifications. The platform has device
limits, session control and audit trails. Details: vacademy.io/security.

## Getting started and switching

Moving from another platform: Vacademy moves users, courses and CRM data in
bulk, at no charge.

Timeline: the portal is usually live within 48 hours of receiving the data
export. Branded mobile apps take 20 to 25 working days.

The subscription starts after go-live. Support is through the dashboard,
WhatsApp and email.

## Things the guide must not claim

No AI lead scoring (scoring is rule-based). No AI for HR. No "personal
learning path". Vacademy does not find leads for an institute. No instant AI
WhatsApp replies. No IndiaMART or JustDial connectors. No class reminders on
WhatsApp. No emotion or psychological analysis of learners. No exam scale
above the tested 3,000 concurrent learners. No client names unless they are
in this file.
