Vacademy Lead Agent

Connect this browser to your Vacademy Lead Agent. It then reads the organisation websites the agent asks for, while Chrome is open.

Advanced

Open Lead Agent → Extension in the Vacademy dashboard to get a code, or click Connect there with this extension installed.


To read organisation websites, the extension needs your permission to open web pages. It only opens the pages the Lead Agent asks for, without your cookies, and never reads your tabs.


The Lead Agent is paused in the dashboard, so there is nothing to read right now.
