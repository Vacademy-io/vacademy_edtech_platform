---
title: "Inbound calls"
description: "Calls your contacts make to your numbers: IVR menus, AI agents and counsellors who answer."
---

An **inbound call** is a call a contact makes **to** one of your numbers: an enquiry line with an IVR menu, a number an [AI agent](/concepts/managing-agents#inbound-numbers) answers, or a number that rings your counsellors. You don't create these calls; you read them from the same `GET /v1/calls` list as the calls you place, with `direction`.

| | Calls you place | Inbound calls |
|---|---|---|
| Created by | `POST /v1/calls` or a batch | The contact, by dialling your number |
| `id` | `call_…` | `inb_…` |
| `object` | `call` | `inbound_call` |
| Listed by `GET /v1/calls` | by default, or with `direction=outbound` / `all` | with `direction=inbound` / `all` |
| Webhooks | `call.completed`, `call.not_connected` | none: read them from the list |

## Listing inbound calls

```bash
curl "https://api.telleo.ai/v1/calls?direction=inbound&created_after=2026-11-27T00:00:00Z" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $TELLEO_API_KEY"
```

| `direction` | Returns |
|---|---|
| `outbound` (default) | The calls you placed through the API, exactly as before. |
| `inbound` | Calls to your numbers, from every IVR menu and number on your account. |
| `all` | Both, in one list, newest first. |

`limit`, `starting_after` and `created_after` work for every `direction` ([Pagination](/platform/pagination)). `batch_id` and `external_id` name calls you placed, so they work only with `direction=outbound`; combining them with `inbound` or `all` returns `400 invalid_request`.

Scope: `calls:read`. Every key that can list calls can list inbound calls.

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">`inb_` followed by the call's id. Use it with `GET /v1/calls/{id}` and as `starting_after`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="object" type="string">Always `inbound_call`, so your code can tell these apart from the calls you placed (`call`).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string">See [Statuses](#statuses).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status_reason" type="string">Why the call was not answered or failed, when your provider gave a reason. Only present when there is one.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="phone_number" type="string | null">The caller's number.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="called_number" type="string | null">Your number that they dialled. `null` when the provider did not report it.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="ivr_selection" type="string | null">The IVR option the caller chose, when the number plays a menu.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="handled_by" type="string | null">`ai_agent` when an AI agent answered, `counsellor` when a counsellor did, `null` when nobody did (a missed call, or the caller hung up in the menu).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="agent_id" type="string | null">The AI agent that answered, when `handled_by` is `ai_agent`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="counsellor_id" type="string | null">The counsellor the call was routed to, when there was one. Matches `id` in `GET /v1/counsellors`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="contact" type="object">`{name}`: the caller's name when the AI agent learned it, else `null`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="crm_lead_linked" type="boolean">`true` when the caller was matched to a lead in your Telleo CRM.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="duration_seconds" type="integer | null">Length of the conversation.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="created_at, answered_at, ended_at" type="string | null">ISO-8601 UTC. `created_at` is when the call came in; `answered_at` when someone picked up; `ended_at` once the call is over.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="recording_url" type="string | null">A temporary link to the recording, valid until `recording_expires_at` (24 hours). Read the call again for a fresh link.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="cost" type="object | null">What the call took from your credits, charge by charge, and the balance after it. `null` while the call is still on. See [Cost of one call](/platform/credits-and-usage#cost-of-one-call).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="outcome" type="object | null">What the AI agent learned, the same block as on an outbound call ([Outcomes](/concepts/outcomes)). `null` when no AI agent answered, or until the agent's report is processed (usually within a minute of hanging up).</ResponseField>

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ringing` | The call came in and is in the IVR menu or ringing; nobody has picked up yet. |
| `in_progress` | An AI agent or a counsellor is talking to the caller. |
| `completed` | The call was answered and has ended. |
| `no_answer` | Nobody picked up (a missed call). |
| `busy` | Your provider reported the call busy. |
| `failed` | The call could not be connected, or no final status came back for it within 2 hours (24 hours on Airtel, whose call records arrive in batches). |
| `canceled` | Your provider reported the call canceled or rejected, typically because the caller hung up before it was connected. |

Inbound calls send no webhooks. To keep your system in step, read `GET /v1/calls?direction=inbound&created_after=…` on a schedule, and read a call again while its status is `ringing` or `in_progress`, or while a `completed` call still has `outcome: null` or `cost.billed: false`.

<Note>
Inbound calls cannot be canceled: `POST /v1/calls/inb_…/cancel` returns `409 call_not_cancelable`.
</Note>

## Billing

An inbound call is billed like an outbound one, at your plan's **inbound** rates: talk time on a Vacademy Voice number, plus AI minutes when an AI agent answered. Calls answered on your own Exotel or Airtel account cost no credits for talk time. A call nobody answered costs nothing. `cost.deductions` lists each charge, as the dashboard's **AI Credits History** shows it.
