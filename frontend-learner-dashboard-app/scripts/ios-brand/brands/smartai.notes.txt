Smart AI Academy is the learner app of Smart AI Academy, a coaching institute in India. It is not an internal app for one organisation: anyone can create an account, browse the course catalogue and enrol.

ACCOUNTS
- Self-registration is open. Tap "Sign up here" under the sign-in form and register with an email address (a one-time code is sent to it), Google, or Sign in with Apple.
- Learners who enrolled with the institute directly receive a username and password and sign in with the "Username" and "Password" fields.
- Account deletion: https://student.smartaiacademy.co.in/delete-user
