Sreedhar's TTS (Triumphant Training Solutions) is the learning app of Sreedhar's TTS, a coaching institute built around one idea: Learn, Practice, Excel.

Create a free account with your email, Google or Apple ID, browse the course catalogue and start learning. If you have already enrolled with the institute, sign in with the same account and your courses will be waiting for you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse the catalogue and enrol in courses such as English and The Engineer's Toolkit
• Learn from video lessons, documents and presentations at your own pace
• Join live instructor-led classes and catch up on recordings
• Practise with quizzes and assessments and see your results instantly
• Track your progress and earn certificates as you complete courses
• Get reminders for upcoming classes and newly added content
• Download lessons to keep learning offline

Learn more at https://learn.ttsedu.co.in
