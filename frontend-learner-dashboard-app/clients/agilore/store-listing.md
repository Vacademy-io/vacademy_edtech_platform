## Full description (~2,850 / 4,000 characters)

Unleash the Power of Learning.

Agilore Global is the official learning app from Agilore Global Private Limited, a specialist Learning & Development partner that helps businesses and corporates design transformative L&D programmes. The app puts your organisation's entire learning journey — courses, live sessions, assessments and coaching — in your pocket.

With 20+ years of L&D expertise across 15+ industries and 10+ countries, Agilore has trained over 50,000 professionals in banking, fintech, technology, retail, startups and sustainability. This app brings that experience directly to your team.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Learn anywhere — Access your courses, video lessons, documents and presentations on your phone or tablet, at your own pace.
• Join live sessions — Attend instructor-led classes and workshops, and catch up with recordings if you miss one.
• Test your understanding — Complete quizzes and assessments, get instant feedback and see where you stand.
• Track your progress — Follow completion, scores and learning streaks across every programme you're enrolled in.
• Earn recognition — Collect certificates as you complete programmes and milestones.
• Stay on track — Get reminders and notifications for upcoming sessions, deadlines and newly assigned content.
• Keep learning offline — Download eligible lessons and continue even when your connection drops.

PROGRAMMES DELIVERED THROUGH AGILORE

• Banking & Finance — Functional expertise, regulatory understanding and operational excellence.
• Leadership Development — Structured programmes that build confident, agile, future-ready leaders.
• Team Effectiveness — Interactive interventions that strengthen collaboration and collective performance.
• Future Skills — Competency-based learning for behavioural and professional skills.
• Digital Mindset — Programmes that foster innovation, digital adaptability and data-driven thinking.
• Foreign Language — Corporate language solutions including Spanish, Mandarin and Japanese.
• Executive Coaching — Confidential, ICF-certified coaching for peak performance and strategic clarity.
• L&D Consulting — Strategic partnership from needs analysis to full learning transformation.

Anyone who wants to learn. Create a free account with your email, Google or Apple ID, browse the programme catalogue and enrol. If your employer has arranged an Agilore programme for you, sign in with the account you were given and it will be waiting for you.
